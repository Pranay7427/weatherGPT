================================================================================
                               PROJECT SYNOPSIS
================================================================================

Project Title : WeatherGPT - AI Weather Assistant & Advisory System
Domain        : Artificial Intelligence & Web Development
Target Users  : Farmers, Travelers, Fishermen, Pilots, General Public

================================================================================
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
Current weather apps have three simple problems:
1. Too Technical: They show confusing charts, graphs, and numbers that normal 
   people cannot easily understand.
2. Not Actionable: They only tell the temperature (e.g., "32°C, Sunny"), but 
   they don't answer real questions like "Can I spray pesticide today?", 
   "Should I carry an umbrella?", or "Is it safe to go fishing?".
3. Language Barrier: Most apps are in English and text-only, making them hard 
   for rural farmers and non-English speakers to use.


================================================================================
2. WHY WE CHOSE THIS PROJECT
================================================================================
- Direct Help to Farmers: Farming depends heavily on rain and temperature. 
  Giving farmers clear advice in their own language saves crops and money.
- Better Decision Making: People need direct answers to daily questions instead 
  of guessing from raw weather numbers.
- Voice Support: Adding voice queries in regional languages helps anyone use 
  the app easily without typing.


================================================================================
3. HOW WE ARE SOLVING IT
================================================================================
WeatherGPT is a smart conversational AI assistant that turns complex weather 
data into simple, everyday advice:

1. Ask in Normal Words (Voice or Text):
   Users can type or speak in 8 Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, 
   Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, English) and get instant answers.

2. Sector-Specific Advice:
   - Agriculture (Kisan): Tells farmers when to irrigate, spray medicine, or sow crops.
   - Everyday Travel: Tells users if they need an umbrella, warm clothes, or if a picnic is fine.
   - Aviation & Marine: Gives pilots and fishermen simple wind, wave, and safety reports.

3. Accurate Multi-Model Forecast:
   Instead of trusting just one forecast, WeatherGPT compares 4 major world 
   weather models (US GFS, European ECMWF, German ICON, WRF) and gives a 
   clear "Confidence Score %".

4. Live Alerts & Radar:
   Shows live rain radar maps and real-time government disaster alerts 
   (cyclones, heatwaves, heavy rain).

5. Dual-Core AI (Fast & Reliable):
   - Fast Built-in Engine: Answers common questions instantly without internet lag or API cost.
   - Smart Cloud AI: Handles complex conversational questions without making up fake weather numbers.


================================================================================
4. TECH STACK
================================================================================
- Backend      : Python, FastAPI (Fast and lightweight web server)
- Frontend     : HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Clean, responsive web page)
- AI Engine    : Custom Rule-Based Engine + Google Gemini API
- Voice Engine : Web Speech API (Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech)
- Maps & Visual: Leaflet.js (Map), Chart.js (Graphs)
- Deployment   : Docker / Local Python Server


================================================================================
5. APIS & DATA SOURCES USED
================================================================================
1. Open-Meteo API     : For live temperature, humidity, wind, rain, and pressure.
2. Weather Models     : NOAA GFS (US), ECMWF (Europe), DWD ICON (Germany).
3. Air Quality (AQI)  : European & US EPA data (PM2.5, pollution levels).
4. RainViewer API     : Live animated Doppler rain radar maps.
5. IMD / WMO Alerts   : Official weather and disaster warnings.
6. OpenStreetMap      : Search location by city or district name.


================================================================================
6. KEY ADVANTAGES
================================================================================
- 100% Free & Lightweight: Works without expensive GPUs or servers.
- Accurate & Safe: Never invents fake weather data.
- Easy for Everyone: Simple language, voice-enabled, works on mobile & desktop.
================================================================================
